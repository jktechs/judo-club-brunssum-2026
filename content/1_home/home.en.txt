# Welcome to Judoclub Brunssum.

Judo is accessible and suitable for everyone, because one can practice this sport at
one's own level with one's own personal goals.

My/our aim is therefore for as many people as possible to get acquainted with
judo. I want to do this in an accessible and open way through
projects, workshops, and demonstrations, and above all through open,
no-obligation access to our club.
-Roy

I regularly coach our competitive judokas at tournaments and
team matches. My goal is to motivate the children and help them get the best out of
themselves. In doing so, I believe that fun should come first.
-Marcel
