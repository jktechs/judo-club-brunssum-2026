# Rules of Judo Club Brunssum

Every club has to deal with rules. Especially at a judo club, rules are of great importance.
Most of these rules relate to safety and hygiene for yourself and others.

The most important rules are:

- Respect each other!
- Ensure safety for yourself and others.
- Keep your judogi (judo suit) clean and intact.
- Make sure your judogi fits well.
- Keep fingernails and toenails short.
- Keep your body clean (personal hygiene).
- Wear flip-flops when standing off the mat.
- Ensure that membership fees are paid on time.

Of course, there are more rules, but these are the most important ones.
